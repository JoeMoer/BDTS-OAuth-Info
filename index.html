BDTS ChatGPT Bridge

Private email and workflow integration operated by BD Trading Solutions.

BDTS ChatGPT Bridge connects an authorised Google Gmail account to an internal workflow system to retrieve operational messages, send authorised responses, organise messages with labels and preserve necessary processing records. The application is used by designated operators; it does not provide a public Gmail account, investment advisory service or customer trading portal.

Google account permissions

The operator may grant the application permission to read email, send mail, modify messages and manage labels. Authorisation is voluntary and can be revoked in the Google account's security settings. Data practices are explained in the privacy policy.

Questions and privacy requests

Use the BD Trading Solutions website contact form. No personal address or telephone number is published on this site.

Scope

This page exists to identify the internal application and explain its Google data handling. It is separate from BD Trading Solutions' subscriber forecasting service and from the private machine-readable forecast feed.
